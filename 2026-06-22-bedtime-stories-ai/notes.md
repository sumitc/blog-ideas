# Notes: Bedtime Stories and AI

## Origin Story
- After a long day of work, bedtime is when kids often ask for one more story.
- Parents may want to keep it magical, but sometimes run out of imagination.
- AI tools can help turn childhood stories and morals into fresh bedtime picture books.

## Published Links
- **Substack**: https://substack.com/home/post/p-203142346

## Core Angle
- Problem: tired parents want to keep bedtime special, but don’t always have a new story ready.
- Insight: AI can act like a creative helper, not a replacement.
- Value: new stories, familiar morals, and a more consistent bedtime ritual.

## Tone
- Warm, reflective, practical
- Personal but broadly relatable
- Focus on parents of young kids
