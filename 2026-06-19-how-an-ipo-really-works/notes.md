## Origin Story
- SpaceX's IPO became the spark for a deeper explanation of how IPOs actually work
- Goal: explain the journey from private ownership to public listing in simple, visual steps
- Core framing: creation, preparation, pricing/allotment, and listing/growth

## Key Storyline
1. **Creation** — shares are created from founder / early investor ownership
2. **Preparation** — bankers, pricing, valuation, and the roadshow
3. **Pricing, Closing, and Allotment** — issue closes, funds are collected, and proceeds are transferred
4. **Listing and Growth** — the stock begins trading and the company enters public-market scrutiny
